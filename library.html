About the author

S. Harrison Knudson is an independent scholar and regulatory policy researcher in Arlington, Virginia. He writes on the New Testament, on the relation of church and state, and on constitutional theory. His constitutional work is collected at semperism.org; he is also Director of the International Foundation for Child-Safe Internet Standards. ORCID 0009-0004-3600-4115. His Virginia law license has been suspended on impairment grounds since July 2024, by consent, while he received medical treatment for injuries from a May 2023 crash; his reinstatement petition is pending (statement).
