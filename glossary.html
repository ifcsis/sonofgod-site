Glossary

Ninety-five terms used on the chart and in the survey, defined in ordinary language and grouped by the spectrum and axis they belong to. Definitions describe what a position holds, not whether it is right. Where a term is contested, or where the chart itself is doing something arguable, that is said rather than smoothed over.
